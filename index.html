THE EXPERIMENT

Forward. Around.
Always in motion.

Start with one beam; another appears every 30 seconds, up to ten. Three form a triangle, four a square, and larger counts form regular polygons. Each new beam has a 50% chance to angle away slightly. Diverging beams widen the oscillation and raise derailment risk. Click a beam to follow it; flight continues throughout the choice. Follow the beams through passing asteroids. Pull the Stabilizer engines lever up or down to gradually narrow or widen the orbit and makes beam lock stronger. At 100%, the ship follows the beams and still transfers between them.

Instability is changing space weather: usually near the middle, occasionally spiking to either extreme. Strong stabilization greatly reduces derailment risk, but cannot eliminate it. If the ship loses beam lock, press Rescue boost to return to the primary beam. Slider changes alone cannot recover lost lock.

High stabilizer power costs more fuel. Glowing blue ring-shaped stations can refuel you: lower stabilizer power below 40% as you approach to leave the beam and settle at the station. Stay nearby for 10 seconds to fill your tank; the station disappears when charging finishes. Refueling also works if you are already derailed nearby. Rescue boost has a 30-second cooldown, costs 8% fuel and takes you back to the primary beam.

The amber trail always records your journey. Drag to explore and scroll to zoom.
