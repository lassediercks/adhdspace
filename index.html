THE EXPERIMENT

Forward. Around.
Always in motion.

Start with one beam; another appears every 30 seconds, up to ten. Three form a triangle, four a square, and larger counts form regular polygons. Follow the beams through passing asteroids. Stabilizer engines gradually narrows the orbit and makes beam lock stronger. At 100%, the ship follows the beams and still transfers between them.

Instability is changing space weather: usually near the middle, occasionally spiking to either extreme. Strong stabilization greatly reduces derailment risk, but cannot eliminate it. If the ship loses beam lock, press Rescue boost to return to the primary beam. Slider changes alone cannot recover lost lock.

High stabilizer power costs more fuel. Mint ring-shaped stations can refuel you: lower stabilizer power below 40% as you approach to leave the beam and settle at the station. Refueling also works if you are already derailed nearby. Rescue boost costs 8% fuel and takes you back to the primary beam.

The amber trail always records your journey. Drag to explore and scroll to zoom.
