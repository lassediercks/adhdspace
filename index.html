THE EXPERIMENT

Forward. Around.
Always in motion.

HX–01 follows a helical trajectory: moving forward while orbiting a beam of light. The camera travels alongside it, so the universe slips past. Use + and − to choose 1–10 parallel beams. Two beams form a figure eight; three form a triangle, four a square, and larger counts form regular polygons. Asteroids always pass by. Instability controls their gravitational pull. At high settings the ship can leave the beam and orbit an asteroid, pausing forward travel. Lower instability below 35% or reset to continue. Coherence narrows the orbit: 100% follows the beams with smooth transfers between them, and 0% gives the widest orbit. The ship still steers around asteroids.
